Invoice collections FAQ

What is automated invoice collections?

Automated invoice collections refreshes your overdue accounts every day, sends reminder texts and emails timed to how late each invoice is, and gives your team a ranked list of who to call. Clockwork Automation builds it on a CRM you own for small businesses in San Diego County.

Will the reminders sound pushy?

No message goes out until you approve its wording. Early reminders are friendly, later ones are firmer, and every message follows rules you set, including opt-outs, quiet hours and which customers never get automated messages.

Does the system make the collection calls?

No. Your team makes the calls, from a ranked list that is ready every morning, so they call the right people first. The system handles the reminders, the payment links and the tracking.

What results can I expect?

At a San Diego portable storage provider, overdue balances over 31 days fell 70%, from $206K to $63K, and total AR fell 55%, from $370K to $167K. The same collector went from about 4 calls a day to 50. Your results depend on your accounts and your team, and we estimate them in the audit.

Where does the overdue data come from?

From the reports your accounting or billing software already produces. We confirm what your system can export on the free call, then set up a daily refresh.

How do customers pay?

Every reminder carries a payment link, so a customer can pay straight from the message. Payments are tracked back to the call or message that earned them.

How long does it take to set up?

Most builds take three to five weeks from signed proposal to live system, including testing with your real data.

How much does it cost?

The first call is free. An automation audit is $500 and is credited toward the build. The build is quoted at a fixed price after the audit, and monthly support starts at $500 per month. Phone platform fees are billed to you directly by the provider.

Who owns the system?

You do. The CRM, the phone account and your customer data are in your name. If you stop working with Clockwork Automation, everything keeps running in your account.
