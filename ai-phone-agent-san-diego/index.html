What your AI phone agent does

The AI phone agent is part of Clockwork Connect, our phones and texting tool. It runs on your business number and logs to the same CRM as the rest of the Clockwork Suite.

Answers every call

Day or night, in a greeting and voice you choose.

Knows your business

Trained on your services, hours, service area and the questions customers ask most.

Captures the lead

Name, number, what they need and how soon, saved to the customer's record before the call ends.

Takes booking requests

It takes a booking request or texts the caller a link to book, and takes a message for anything else.

Hands off to a person

Emergencies, upset customers and anything off-script go to the right person or the on-call phone.

Leaves a summary

Every call gets a short written summary on the customer's record, so nothing lives on one person's phone.

You set the rules

You decide which calls it handles.

Calls the agent can take

  • Hours, pricing ranges and service area
  • New customer details and what they need
  • Booking requests, with a link texted to book
  • After-hours and overflow calls
  • "Did you get my message?" status calls

Calls that go to a person

  • Emergencies, under the on-call rule you set
  • Upset or confused callers
  • Billing disputes and anything legal
  • Your best customers, if you want them routed straight through
  • Anyone who asks for a human

Your agent tells every caller up front that it's an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded. California requires consent from everyone on a recorded call, so the disclosure is built into the greeting.

AI phone agent FAQ

What is an AI phone agent?

An AI phone agent is an automated assistant that answers your business calls in a natural voice, answers common questions, takes the caller's details and passes calls to your team when needed. Clockwork Automation sets one up and trains it on your business for small businesses across San Diego County.

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. The greeting tells every caller it's an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded. Being upfront builds trust, and California's recording rules call for it.

What if a caller needs a real person?

You choose when calls go to a person: emergencies, upset callers, specific customers or anyone who asks. Those calls ring through to your team or your on-call phone.

Can it book appointments?

It takes a booking request or texts the caller a link to book. Your team keeps control of the calendar and confirms the time.

Can I hear it before I commit?

Yes. On your free 30-minute call we ring our own AI phone agent live, so you hear how it greets callers, answers questions and hands off to a person, then see the call summary land in the CRM.

Can we keep our current phone number?

Usually yes. We can move your existing number to a business phone platform that supports texting and an AI phone agent, or set up a new local number. We check what fits on the free call.

Does it replace my front desk?

For most businesses it covers the calls nobody can get to: after hours, overflow and the same everyday questions. Your team keeps the calls that need a person.

How much does an AI phone agent cost?

The first call is free. An automation audit is $500 and is credited toward the build. Setup and training are quoted at a fixed price after the audit, and monthly support starts at $500 per month. Phone platform and AI usage fees are billed to you directly by the provider.

Who owns the system?

You do. The phone account, CRM and call data are in your name. If you stop working with Clockwork Automation, everything keeps running in your account.
