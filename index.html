Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What does a business automation consultant do?

A business automation consultant finds repetitive work that costs a business time or money, such as chasing late payments or following up on quotes, and builds systems that do that work automatically. Clockwork Automation builds those systems for small businesses in San Diego County.

How much does business automation cost for a small business?

The first call is free. An automation audit is $500 and is credited toward a build. Builds are quoted at a fixed price after the audit, and monthly support starts at $500 per month.

How long does an automation build take?

Most builds take three to five weeks from signed proposal to live system, including testing with your real data.

Do I need to buy new software?

Most systems are built on a CRM (customer relationship manager) in an account you own, which usually costs under $20 per user per month. If you already use software that can do the job, we can build there instead.

Who owns the system after it's built?

You own your system, your account and your data. If you stop working with Clockwork Automation, everything keeps running in your account.

Will automated messages say the wrong thing to my customers?

No message goes out until you approve its wording. Every text and email follows rules you set, including opt-outs, quiet hours and which customers never get automated messages.

What is fractional operations?

Fractional operations means having a part-time head of operations without a full-time hire. Clockwork Automation runs a weekly operations meeting, reviews your dashboard each month, fixes processes hands-on and coaches your team leads, for $2,500 to $4,000 per month.

Do you set up our phone system?

Yes. Clockwork Automation sets up and supports your business phone numbers and texting, and connects them to your CRM hub so every call and text is logged to the right customer automatically. We can also set up and train an AI phone agent to answer your calls.

Can an AI phone agent answer our calls?

Yes. Clockwork Automation sets up and trains an AI phone agent on your business: your hours, services and the questions customers ask most. It answers calls day and night, tells callers it is an AI assistant, takes their details and logs each call to your CRM, and passes anything it should not handle to your team. We review real call summaries with you and tune it after launch.

Can I start with just one tool?

Yes. Pick whichever tool fits your business. On the first call we figure out together which one will pay off fastest. Every tool plugs into the same hub, so nothing gets rebuilt when you add more.

Do you only work with San Diego businesses?

Clockwork Automation is based in San Diego and meets clients in person across San Diego County. Businesses elsewhere can work together remotely.
