Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What is missed-call text-back?

Missed-call text-back sends an automatic text to anyone whose call you miss, so they know you will call back and don't move on to the next company. Clockwork Automation sets it up on your business numbers.

Will it work with our current phone numbers?

Usually yes. We can keep your existing numbers or set up a business phone platform that supports texting and logs every call and text to the customer's record.

Can after-hours calls be handled automatically?

Yes. After-hours callers get an immediate text and land on a list for your team in the morning, or you can route true emergencies to whoever is on call. An AI phone agent can also answer after-hours calls and take the details.

Can an AI phone agent answer our calls?

Yes. Clockwork Automation sets up and trains an AI phone agent on your business: your hours, services and the questions customers ask most. It answers calls day and night, tells callers it is an AI assistant, takes their details and logs each call to your CRM, and passes anything it should not handle to your team. We review real call summaries with you and tune it after launch.

How much does it cost?

The first call is free. An automation audit is $500 and is credited toward the build. The build is quoted at a fixed price after the audit, and monthly support starts at $500 per month. Phone platform fees are billed to you directly by the provider.

Do you set up and support the phone system?

Yes. Clockwork Automation sets up and supports your business phone numbers and texting, and connects them to your CRM so every call and text is logged.
