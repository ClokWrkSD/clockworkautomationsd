Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Clockwork Automation ("we," "us") provides business automation services in San Diego County, California. This policy explains what information we collect through clockworkautomationsd.com, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email address, mobile phone number, business name, and the numbers you enter in our calculator.
  • Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as browser type, pages visited, and approximate location, collected through standard website analytics.
  • Information from calls and texts with us, such as the content of messages you send.

How we use it

  • To send you your calculator report and reply to your questions.
  • To contact you by phone, text message, and email about our services, including marketing, if you agreed to that.
  • To schedule and run consultations, and to improve our website and services.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive messages, we may send you texts about your report and our services, including marketing. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

To stop receiving texts, reply STOP at any time. For help, reply HELP or contact us at the details below. We will confirm your opt-out with one final message.

Mobile information, including phone numbers and text message opt-in consent, is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell your information.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our phone and texting platform, CRM, email, scheduling, and website hosting providers. They may use it only to provide services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law. This sharing does not include mobile opt-in data or consent for third-party marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep information as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, opt out of marketing email using the unsubscribe link, and ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by contacting us. California residents have additional rights under California law, including the right to know what we collect and to request deletion. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising these rights.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new effective date above.
