Questions owners ask

Lead follow-up FAQ

What is automated lead follow-up?

Automated lead follow-up sends timely texts and emails to every new lead and every open quote until the lead books, declines or asks you to stop. Clockwork Automation builds it on a CRM you own, using wording you approve, for small businesses in San Diego County.

Will the messages sound like me?

No message goes out until you approve its wording. We write the messages with you in your voice, and every text and email follows rules you set, including opt-outs, quiet hours and which customers never get automated messages.

What makes the follow-up stop?

A booking, a decline, a stop request or a reply that needs a person. Replies that need an answer go to your team, so the sequence never talks over a real conversation.

Does it use texts or email?

Both. We set up the mix with you based on how your customers respond. Texting runs on your business number, and every message is logged on the customer's record.

What does it connect to?

Most systems are built on a CRM in an account you own, which usually costs under $20 per user per month. If you already use software that can do the job, we can build there instead.

Can it also ask for reviews and rebook repeat customers?

Yes. Review requests go out after finished jobs, and rebooking reminders go to repeat customers when they are due. You choose which jobs and customers get them.

How long does it take to set up?

Most builds take three to five weeks from signed proposal to live system, including testing with your real data.

How much does it cost?

The first call is free. An automation audit is $500 and is credited toward the build. The build is quoted at a fixed price after the audit, and monthly support starts at $500 per month. Phone platform fees are billed to you directly by the provider.

Who owns the system?

You do. The CRM, the phone account and your customer data are in your name. If you stop working with Clockwork Automation, everything keeps running in your account.
