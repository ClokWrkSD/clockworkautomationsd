Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: October 22, 2026

Clockwork Automation wants everyone to be able to use clockworkautomationsd.com, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, voice control, magnification, or other assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We build with semantic HTML, keep text and controls at readable contrast, make every feature usable by keyboard, label form fields, and respect your device's reduced-motion and dark-mode settings.

How we check

We review our pages with automated checks and manual testing, including keyboard-only navigation, and we re-check whenever we change the site.

Known limitations

We are not aware of any open accessibility barriers on the site. Accessibility is ongoing work, and a page or feature may still fall short in a way we have not caught.

Tell us about a problem

If something on our site is hard to use, or you need information in another format, please contact us. Tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the device or assistive technology you use if you are comfortable sharing it. We will work with you and fix confirmed problems as quickly as we can.
