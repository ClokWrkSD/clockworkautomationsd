Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

How do I get more Google reviews for my cleaning company or med spa?

Ask every client, every time, at the right moment. Clockwork Automation sends review requests automatically after each visit, a few days apart, and stops as soon as the client leaves one.

Can rebooking reminders be automated?

Yes. Reminders go out when each client is due for their next service, based on how often they usually book, with a link to book again.

Will this reduce no-shows?

Automatic confirmations and reminders before each appointment are one of the most common ways businesses reduce no-shows. We set the timing with you.

Do you only send review requests to happy clients?

No. Every request includes your public review link, alongside an easy way to send private feedback. Google's policies don't allow sending only happy customers to reviews.
