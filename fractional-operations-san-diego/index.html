Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What is fractional operations?

Fractional operations means having a part-time head of operations without a full-time hire. Clockwork Automation runs a weekly operations meeting, reviews your dashboard each month, fixes processes hands-on and coaches your team leads.

How much does a fractional operations manager cost?

Clockwork Automation's fractional operations service costs $2,500 to $4,000 per month, depending on scope, which includes about 10 hours of hands-on work each month.

Who is fractional operations for?

Owner-run businesses with about 5 to 25 people where the owner is the bottleneck, but a full-time operations manager isn't justified yet.

What tools do you use?

Your operations run on a CRM (customer relationship manager) you own, connected to the Clockwork Suite: collections, follow-up, phones and a weekly dashboard.

Do you work in person?

Yes, across San Diego County, and by video for businesses elsewhere.
